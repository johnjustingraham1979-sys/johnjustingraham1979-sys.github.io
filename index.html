Fablevyr Studio makes original animated series, illustrated stories and researched explainers. Each of our channels is its own brand with its own audience and voice; Fablevyr is the production company behind them.

Our channels
  • Snugwillow — gentle stories for children 4–7, made to watch together with a grown-up.
  • Chapter & Lantern — classic books and original tales, brought to life.
  • More channels for teens, adults and curious minds are in development.

How we make things

Every story is written, designed and reviewed by our studio. We use AI tools as part of production — for drafts, illustration, voices and animation — and every release is checked and approved by a person before it is published. We label synthetic or altered content where platforms require it.

The Fablevyr Studio app

“Fablevyr Studio” is also the name of our private production software. It connects only to YouTube channels that Fablevyr owns, so that finished, approved episodes can be uploaded and scheduled. It is not offered to the public. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what it accesses.
