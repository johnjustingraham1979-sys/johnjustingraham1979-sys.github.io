Who we are

Fablevyr Studio (“Fablevyr”, “we”) is an independent animation studio that publishes videos on channels it owns, including Snugwillow and Chapter & Lantern. Contact: fablevyr.studio@outlook.com.

Viewers of our videos

We do not run accounts, sign-ups, comments, ads or tracking on this website, and we do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos. Videos are hosted by YouTube (and, in future, other video platforms); those platforms process data under their own privacy policies — for YouTube, see the Google Privacy Policy. Channels made for children are published with YouTube’s “made for kids” setting, which limits data collection and disables personalized ads and comments on those videos.

We may see aggregated, anonymous channel statistics that platforms provide to all creators (for example total views). We do not receive or store individual viewers’ identities.

The Fablevyr Studio app and Google user data

The Fablevyr Studio app is our private production software. It is used only by Fablevyr and connects only to YouTube channels that Fablevyr owns. When a channel is connected, the app requests these Google permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload) — to upload finished, approved episodes and set their title, description, schedule, audience (“made for kids”) and synthetic-content labels, and thumbnails.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) — to confirm which channel a connection belongs to before every upload (so a video can never be sent to the wrong channel), and to check whether an upload already exists before retrying.

How it is stored: the connection token is stored only on Fablevyr’s own computer, encrypted with the operating system’s protected storage. It is never sent to any server other than Google’s, never sold or shared, and never used for advertising.

What it does not do: it does not read or download viewers’ personal data, comments or messages; it does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any non-YouTube data; and it does not use Google user data to train AI models.

Limited Use: Fablevyr Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: access can be revoked at any time from the Google account’s Third-party access page, and the app also deletes its stored token when a channel is disconnected.

Children

Some of our channels are made for children. We never knowingly collect personal information from children, and our own website and app have no features for children to submit information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
